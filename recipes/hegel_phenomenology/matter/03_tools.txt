# A Short Timeline

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1770 | Hegel born in Stuttgart (27 August). |
| 1781 | Kant, *Critique of Pure Reason*. |
| 1788–93 | Hegel at the Tübingen seminary with Hölderlin and Schelling. |
| 1789 | The French Revolution begins. |
| 1794 | Fichte, *Foundations of the Entire Science of Knowledge*. |
| 1800 | Schelling, *System of Transcendental Idealism*. |
| 1801 | Hegel moves to Jena; publishes *The Difference between Fichte’s and Schelling’s System of Philosophy*. |
| 1802–3 | Hegel and Schelling edit the *Critical Journal of Philosophy*. |
| 1806 | Battle of Jena (14 October); Hegel completes the *Phenomenology*. |
| 1807 | *Phenomenology of Spirit* published. |
| 1812–16 | *Science of Logic*. |
| 1817 | *Encyclopaedia of the Philosophical Sciences*. |
| 1818 | Hegel becomes professor in Berlin. |
| 1820 | *Elements of the Philosophy of Right*. |
| 1831 | Hegel dies in Berlin (14 November). |
